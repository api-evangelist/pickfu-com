PickFu is a consumer research platform that connects businesses with real people to gather quick, actionable feedback on products, designs, copy, and more. Founded in 2008, the service offers synthetic and real audience panels, AI‑enhanced insights, and a range of integrations for marketers, developers, and product teams. Over 3 million responses have been collected across e‑commerce, gaming, publishing, and marketing sectors, helping thousands of customers make data‑driven decisions.

**Areas:** Consumer Research · Marketing · AI · Surveys · Real People

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for PickFu: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
